Diving to the Bottom of the Ocean

The Sunlight Zone (0 - 200m)

This is the top part of the ocean. Sunlight can reach here so plants and plankton can grow. Lots of fish, turtles and dolphins live here.

The Twilight Zone

The Twilight Zone (200 - 1000m)

It starts to get really dark here. Only a little bit of light gets through.

Some fun facts:

  • A lot of animals swim up to the surface at night to eat
  • Some fish have really big eyes to see in the dark
The Titanic - 3800m

The Midnight Zone (1000 - 4000m)

There is no sunlight at all down here. The Titanic is about 3800m deep, which is in this zone.

Lots of animals down here make their own light.

Challenger Deep - 10,935m

The Bottom

The deepest part of the ocean is called Challenger Deep, in the Mariana Trench. In 2012 James Cameron went all the way down by himself in a submarine called the Deepsea Challenger.
